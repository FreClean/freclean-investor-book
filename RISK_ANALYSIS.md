# Risk Analysis

*Standalone excerpt of Part XVI of the full book. See [`book/chapters/16-risk.md`](book/chapters/16-risk.md) for the version embedded in context with the rest of the analysis.*

## Risk Summary

| Category | Summary |
|---|---|
| Market/macro | Haiti's economy contracted for a 7th consecutive year in 2025 (-2.7% GDP), with 28.3% inflation and ~49% of the population below US$3/day (World Bank), a real constraint on domestic spending, independent of FreClean's execution |
| Competition | Faces informal/low-cost labor, international product brands, and regional service companies simultaneously, see `book/chapters/09-competitive-landscape.md` |
| Supply chain | Haiti's broader logistics and security environment, described by the World Bank as affected by "intensifying gang violence" as of 2025, is a material operating-environment risk for any physical-goods business |
| Manufacturing | No confirmed facility, process, or QC system, the entire product-revenue line remains a plan, not a capability |
| Regulatory (products) | Consumer product safety/labeling status Not Yet Verified for any product, any jurisdiction |
| Regulatory (crypto payments) | KYC/AML and refund posture for USDm/CELO not yet addressed publicly |
| Financial | No published financial data, primarily an informational risk until real reporting exists |
| Operational | Widest gap in the entire book between stated ambition and demonstrated capability, see `book/chapters/17-what-still-needs-to-be-proven.md` |
| Technology | Limited near-term exposure (no live production system yet); risk shifts once `freclean-api` and a production admin/website are built |
| Brand | Low, relative to other categories, the primary risk is a mismatch between brand promise and unverified operational reality |
| Geographic expansion | Expanding before Haiti operations are validated would compound execution risk across multiple regulatory/logistics environments |

## Risk Mitigation Framework

| Risk category | Mitigation approach |
|---|---|
| Market/macro | Prioritize diaspora-linked, less domestically-cyclical revenue where possible |
| Competition | Lean into verified differentiators (brand, entrepreneurship platform) rather than competing on price alone |
| Manufacturing | Validate service-line economics before committing large manufacturing capital |
| Regulatory (products) | Commission Safety Data Sheets and confirm labeling requirements before launch |
| Regulatory (crypto) | Formalize KYC/AML and refund policy before scaling that payment channel |
| Financial | Establish basic financial reporting before any funding conversation |
| Operational | Treat the gap list in `book/chapters/17-what-still-needs-to-be-proven.md` as a literal build checklist |
| Geographic | Sequence expansion strictly behind Haiti operational validation |

See [`book/chapters/16-risk.md`](book/chapters/16-risk.md) for the full narrative treatment of each category, and [`sources/REFERENCES.md`](sources/REFERENCES.md) for the Haiti macroeconomic citations used above.
