## What This Document Is and Is Not

FreClean has not published revenue, cost, or margin data. **(TBD / Not Yet Verified.)** This document presents the confirmed revenue model structure alongside clearly labeled **Illustrative Scenarios**, worked examples built on stated assumptions, meant only to demonstrate the calculation method. No number here is real FreClean data.

## Revenue Model Structure

Service Revenue (residential, commercial, specialized contracts), Product Revenue (retail, wholesale), Manufacturing Revenue (white-label, private-label), and Franchise & Licensing Revenue (franchise fees, training/consulting).

## Break-Even Framework

Requires fixed cost data not yet published. **(TBD.)** Structurally, a services-led revenue strategy reaches break-even faster than a manufacturing-led strategy, since it avoids large upfront inventory and production capital.

## Scenario Analysis

| Scenario | Illustrative assumption | Directional implication |
|---|---|---|
| Conservative | Slow acquisition, services-only for 12+ months | Lower near-term capital need |
| Base | Services scale first; limited-SKU product launch in Year 1 | Matches FreClean's stated Phase 1 roadmap |
| Accelerated | Rapid franchise/wholesale signing alongside scaling | Higher capital need and execution risk |
