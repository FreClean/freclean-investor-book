# Part XVII: What Still Needs to Be Proven

## Purpose of This Chapter

This chapter exists because a serious investor publication earns trust by stating its own limits plainly, not by hiding them behind confident language elsewhere in the book. Every item below has already been flagged individually in its relevant chapter; this chapter exists to put them all in one place so nothing is lost in the narrative.

## Unvalidated Assumptions

- That customers who receive a FreClean service go on to purchase FreClean products (the "ecosystem loop," Part VII): plausible, structurally reasonable, but **not measured**.
- That FreClean's twelve stated target segments (Part V) can be pursued without an explicit near-term prioritization: this book's own analysis (Part V) suggests otherwise, but FreClean has not stated a formal priority order.
- That the CeloHT relationship provides meaningful customer-acquisition or credibility benefit (Part II, Part XI): a reasonable hypothesis, not a confirmed outcome.

## Missing Data

- Any customer count, retention rate, repeat-purchase rate, or satisfaction data (Part V).
- Any revenue, cost, or margin figures for any part of the business (Part XIV).
- Any confirmed active service area or current operating capacity (Part IV).

## Missing Certifications

- No manufacturing, quality (e.g., ISO), or safety certification has been confirmed for any product (Part III, Part X).
- No regulatory approval for any product in any jurisdiction has been confirmed (Part XVI).

## Operational Gaps

- No confirmed manufacturing facility, capacity, or process (Part X).
- No confirmed inventory location or system in live use (Part X).
- No confirmed distribution or logistics partner (Part XIII).
- No confirmed formal quality-control checklist or customer-feedback loop (Part IV, Part X).

## Product Gaps

- No product has confirmed pricing, packaging, size variants, or ingredient/safety data (Part III).
- No product has confirmed active-sale status (Part III).

## Financial Gaps

- No revenue, cost structure, margin, or funding-history data exists (Part XIV, Part XV).
- No official funding target has been set (Part XV).

## Market Validation Gaps

- No Haiti- or Caribbean-specific market-size figure exists from a credible third-party source (Part VIII).
- No customer research or demand validation has been published (Part II, Part V).

## Distribution Gaps

- No confirmed active retail, wholesale, or franchise partner exists (Part XIII).
- No confirmed e-commerce or online ordering capability exists (Part XI).

## Technology Gaps

- No live backend (`freclean-api`), database, or authentication system exists (Part XI).
- The admin platform exists only as a specification and an offline UI prototype using fictional data (Part XI).

## Team Gaps

- No confirmed organizational structure, headcount, or named leadership team has been published (Part X). A *proposed*, not confirmed, role structure exists in the `freclean-admin` specification.

## What Would Most Efficiently Close These Gaps

If FreClean's team can supply only a small number of things before a serious funding conversation, the highest-leverage items are: (1) a confirmed near-term service area and current operating status, (2) at minimum, directional cost/pricing data for one service and one product, and (3) a stated near-term priority segment (Part V). These three alone would allow Part XIV's Illustrative Scenarios to become a real financial framework.
