## About This Book

This is the official FreClean Investor Book: a self-contained publication intended to let a reader who knows nothing about FreClean understand the company clearly, from its mission and brand through its market, business model, operations, financials, risks, and investment thesis.

Every claim in this book is labeled by verification status: **Confirmed**, **Planned**, **TBD**, **Not Yet Verified**, **Not Publicly Disclosed**, **Concept**, **Illustrative Scenario**, or **Assumption**. See [Appendix A](book/appendices/appendix-a-verification-status-legend.md) for the full definitions. This labeling is applied consistently, and it is the book's central discipline. No claim is dressed up as more certain than the evidence supports, and no weakness is hidden; Part XVII is dedicated entirely to what remains unproven.

This book was compiled from FreClean's own published organizational materials and from named, cited third-party market research (see [Sources and References](sources/REFERENCES.md)). No fact about FreClean was invented. Where information was unavailable, this book says so explicitly.

## A Note on Independence

This book is a standalone publication. It does not depend on, reference, or require any other FreClean repository to be read or understood: every fact it relies on is restated directly within its own chapters.

## Reading Order

The nineteen parts above are written to be read in order, each ending with a short bridge to the next. A reader in a hurry can instead start with [`EXECUTIVE_SUMMARY.md`](EXECUTIVE_SUMMARY.md) and [`INVESTMENT_THESIS.md`](INVESTMENT_THESIS.md), then go directly to Part XVII, *What Still Needs to Be Proven*, before deciding whether to read the rest.
