# Executive Summary

FreClean is a Haiti-based professional cleaning company building a broader ecosystem: cleaning services, manufactured cleaning products, entrepreneurship and franchise programs, and digital payment infrastructure delivered through an affiliated Web3 initiative called CeloHT. **(Confirmed, as published by FreClean.)**

## The Opportunity

FreClean sits at the intersection of two very different things. The first is a large, structurally growing global market: independent research places the global household cleaning products market for 2025 somewhere between roughly USD 42 billion and USD 282 billion, depending on how narrowly or broadly a given firm defines the category (see [`sources/REFERENCES.md`](sources/REFERENCES.md)). The second is a specific, differentiated position within that market: a Haitian and Caribbean-rooted brand, a genuine entrepreneurship and franchise platform, and a digital payments strategy built around USDm and CELO that almost no cleaning business offers. Haiti's diaspora remits an estimated USD 3.8 to 4.1 billion a year, roughly 20% of the country's GDP. This book treats that flow as strategically relevant to FreClean's positioning, though it is worth being clear that no published FreClean strategy document draws that connection explicitly; it is this book's own observation.

## What FreClean Has Built

A clearly articulated mission, vision, and twelve core values. A diversified revenue model spanning eleven distinct lines. A published catalog covering seven product categories and eighteen named products. A detailed, twenty-three item cleaning service menu across residential, commercial, and specialized categories. A professionally developed brand identity. And a full specification, plus a working UI prototype, for an internal operations platform.

## What FreClean Has Not Yet Built

No product carries confirmed pricing, packaging, ingredients, or active sale status. No manufacturing facility, inventory system, or distribution partner has been confirmed. No financial data, whether revenue, cost, or margin, has been published anywhere. No backend technology exists behind the admin platform specification; it remains a design reference. This book treats these gaps as central to the analysis rather than something to work around. The full list is in `book/chapters/17-what-still-needs-to-be-proven.md`.

## The Thesis, in Brief

FreClean combines a real, differentiated market position with a substantial execution gap, documented honestly rather than hidden. The investment case rests less on the strength of the narrative and more on whether that gap can actually be closed, starting with operational proof, meaning a confirmed and active service business, and a minimum viable product line. The full treatment, including the specific conditions that would strengthen or invalidate this thesis, is in `INVESTMENT_THESIS.md`.
