**What is the role of cryptocurrency in FreClean's business?**
FreClean accepts USDm, a dollar-pegged stablecoin (formerly known as cUSD, renamed by the Mento Protocol in late 2025), alongside CELO, the more volatile native token of the Celo network. Customers would typically hold and send either through the Valora wallet. FreClean draws on this payment infrastructure through its relationship with CeloHT, a separate Haitian Web3 initiative. No transaction volume, KYC or anti-money-laundering policy, or handling of CELO's price volatility has been published, so this should be read as a stated capability, not a proven one. See [`book/chapters/11-technology.md`](book/chapters/11-technology.md).

**What are the biggest risks?**
Haiti's macroeconomic environment, unresolved manufacturing capability, and the general gap between FreClean's stated ambition and demonstrated operational capability. See [`RISK_ANALYSIS.md`](RISK_ANALYSIS.md).

**Does this book recommend investing in FreClean?**
