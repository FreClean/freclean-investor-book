## What Is FreClean?

FreClean is a Haiti-based professional cleaning company building an ecosystem combining cleaning services, manufactured cleaning products, entrepreneurship/franchise programs, and digital/Web3 payment infrastructure through an affiliated initiative called CeloHT. **(Confirmed.)**

## Origin and Name

The name **FreClean** combines "Fre" (from "Freda") and "Clean" (from the Haitian Creole "Pwòp," meaning clean at every level: conscious and economic). **(Confirmed**: provided directly by FreClean.) No founder narrative, company history, or founding date has been published. **(Not Publicly Disclosed.)**

## Mission

> "Our mission is to provide reliable, affordable, and environmentally responsible cleaning products and professional services while empowering individuals through entrepreneurship, education, innovation, and digital financial inclusion. We strive to improve everyday living by delivering exceptional quality, creating sustainable employment opportunities, and supporting local economic development."

## Vision

> "Our vision is to become one of the Caribbean's most trusted and innovative cleaning brands, recognized internationally for excellence, sustainability, customer satisfaction, and technological innovation."

## Core Values

Excellence, Integrity, Professionalism, Innovation, Sustainability, Transparency, Accountability, Customer Satisfaction, Community Development, Environmental Responsibility, Continuous Improvement, Collaboration.

## Positioning

FreClean positions itself at the intersection of a consumer cleaning brand, an entrepreneurship/franchise platform, and a Web3-enabled payments strategy: a combination that differentiates it from single-line competitors (see `MARKET_ANALYSIS.md` and `book/chapters/09-competitive-landscape.md`) if executed well.

## Business Overview

FreClean is based in Haiti with stated expansion intent into the Dominican Republic, the wider Caribbean, and eventually international markets. **(Confirmed** intention; **TBD** timeline.) It operates, or intends to operate, across residential, commercial, and specialized cleaning services; a seven-category manufactured product line; wholesale and white/private-label manufacturing; and franchise and entrepreneurship programs: each examined in full in the corresponding chapter of [`INVESTOR_BOOK.md`](INVESTOR_BOOK.md).
