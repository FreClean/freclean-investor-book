# Product Visuals

## ⚠️ CONCEPT VISUAL: NOT FINAL PRODUCT PHOTOGRAPHY

Both files in this folder are original, schematic vector illustrations: not photographs, and not depictions of any real, manufactured FreClean container or label design. They were built directly from FreClean's confirmed brand colors and are reused here (originally produced for the `freclean-products` repository) purely to give this investor book a visual sense of product direction, consistent with the instruction to use premium concept visuals: clearly labeled: where authentic product photography does not exist.

| File | Depicts | Status |
|---|---|---|
| `bottle-mockup-multipurpose.svg` | A generic bottle silhouette labeled for the Multi-Purpose Cleaner product | CONCEPT VISUAL |
| `product-lineup-concept.svg` | Three concept bottle silhouettes arranged as a product family | CONCEPT VISUAL / MOCKUP |

No FreClean product currently has confirmed packaging, and no authentic product photography exists; see Part III of the book (`book/chapters/03-products.md`) and the `freclean-products` repository for full detail.
