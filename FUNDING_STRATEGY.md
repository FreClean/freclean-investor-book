# Funding Strategy

*Standalone excerpt of Part XV of the full book. See [`book/chapters/15-funding.md`](book/chapters/15-funding.md) for the version with the full capital-flow diagram.*

## No Official Funding Target Exists

FreClean has not published a specific capital-raise target, valuation, or funding round structure. **(TBD / Not Publicly Disclosed.)** This document presents a capital deployment framework instead of an invented target.

## Capital Deployment Priority Order

Based on the gap analysis across the Products, Operations, and Technology chapters of the full book, capital would most productively address gaps in roughly this order:

1. **Manufacturing**: moving the 18-product catalog from "named and categorized" to "in development," including real formulation and Safety Data Sheets.
2. **Working capital / inventory**: once manufacturing exists, initial inventory to fulfill orders reliably.
3. **Technology**: building `freclean-api` and a production version of the `freclean-admin` platform.
4. **Distribution**: establishing the first confirmed retail or wholesale channel.
5. **Marketing/brand activation**: the brand foundation is already strong; capital here activates rather than builds it.
6. **Team**: formalizing roles beyond the currently proposed (not confirmed) structure.

This ordering is this book's own analysis, not a FreClean-published capital allocation plan.

## Working Capital Considerations

A manufacturing- and inventory-dependent business model is more working-capital-intensive than a pure services business. Capital that builds *capability* (manufacturing setup, technology) should be distinguished from capital that funds *ongoing working capital* (inventory turns, payroll): the latter should be sized against confirmed unit economics, which do not yet exist (see [`FINANCIAL_FRAMEWORK.md`](FINANCIAL_FRAMEWORK.md)).

## What FreClean Would Need to Provide

For any investor conversation to move from this framework to an actual funding ask: a specific near-term service-area and product-launch plan, at least directional manufacturing cost data, and a specific team/hiring plan. None currently exist in public materials.
