## Global Market Size

Six independent market-research publishers size the global household cleaning products market for 2025 at figures ranging from approximately **USD 41.7 billion to USD 282.3 billion**, with forecast CAGRs clustering between roughly 4.4% and 6.5% through the early-to-mid 2030s. **(Confirmed, third-party sourced.)** See [`charts/market-size-estimates-range.png`](charts/market-size-estimates-range.png) for a visual comparison. The wide range reflects differing product-scope definitions rather than genuine analyst disagreement about a single figure.

## TAM / SAM / SOM

- **TAM:** the full global cleaning products and services category: hundreds of billions of dollars annually. Directionally accurate but not a meaningful planning number for an early-stage, single-country company.
- **SAM:** no third-party publisher breaks out a Haiti- or Caribbean-specific cleaning market figure. **(TBD**: this book declines to fabricate one by simple population-scaling, since that method is not reliable enough to present as a market size.)
- **SOM:** requires a defined service area, confirmed pricing, and a realistic capture-rate assumption: none of which FreClean has published. **(TBD.)**

## Haiti: Macroeconomic Context

Haiti's population is approximately 11.9–12.0 million (2025 estimates); nominal GDP is estimated at roughly USD 24–34 billion depending on methodology (World Bank, Trading Economics, and other sources vary; see References for the full range and why it exists). Haiti's real GDP contracted for a seventh consecutive year in 2025 (-2.7%), inflation averaged 28.3%, and an estimated 49.0% of the population lived on less than US$3.00/day (World Bank, 2025). **(Confirmed, third-party sourced.)**

## The Diaspora Opportunity

Haiti's diaspora remitted approximately USD 3.8–4.1 billion in 2023–2024: roughly 20% of Haiti's GDP and the country's primary source of foreign currency (IHSI/The Haitian Times; Trading Economics). **(Confirmed, third-party sourced.)** This is a large, recurring capital flow structurally distinct from constrained domestic discretionary spending, and may be strategically relevant to FreClean's stated interest in digital/Web3 payments: a connection this book raises as its own analytical observation, not a confirmed FreClean strategy.

## Dominican Republic and Wider Caribbean

FreClean has stated expansion intent but published no market-sizing, timeline, or entry strategy for either market. **(Confirmed** intention; **TBD** everything else.) No credible Dominican Republic- or Caribbean-region-specific cleaning products market figure was located during this research.

## Bottom Line

FreClean operates in a large and growing global category, in a home market with genuine macroeconomic headwinds and a genuinely large, distinct diaspora capital flow. A credible SAM/SOM for FreClean specifically does not yet exist and should be built from FreClean's own confirmed service-area and pricing data once available, not estimated top-down from global figures.
