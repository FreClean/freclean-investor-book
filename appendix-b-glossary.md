# Appendix B: Glossary

**CAGR**: Compound Annual Growth Rate; the year-over-year growth rate of a value over a specified period.

**CeloHT**: A community-driven initiative promoting financial inclusion, digital innovation, entrepreneurship, environmental sustainability, and Web3 education across Haiti, the Dominican Republic, and the Caribbean. FreClean states it is "powered by" CeloHT. See Part XI.

**Celo**: A blockchain network. FreClean accepts two Celo-based assets, USDm and CELO, as payment.

**USDm (Mento Dollar)**: A US-dollar-pegged stablecoin issued by the Mento Protocol on the Celo blockchain. USDm was known as cUSD (Celo Dollar) until the Mento Protocol renamed its stablecoin family in late 2025; this book uses USDm throughout, since that is the current name, and notes the earlier name only where FreClean's own historical materials require it.

**CELO**: The native, price-fluctuating asset of the Celo blockchain, distinct from USDm.

**LTV**: Customer Lifetime Value; the total gross margin a business expects to earn from a customer over the relationship.

**Private label**: Manufacturing goods to a retailer's specification for sale under the retailer's own brand.

**SAM**: Serviceable Addressable Market; the portion of the Total Addressable Market that a company's business model and geography could realistically reach.

**SDS**: Safety Data Sheet; a standardized document describing a chemical product's hazards, handling, and safety information.

**SKU**: Stock-Keeping Unit; a unique identifier for a specific product/variant.

**SOM**: Serviceable Obtainable Market; the portion of the SAM a company could realistically capture given its actual resources and competitive position.

**TAM**: Total Addressable Market; the total market demand for a product or service category.

**Unit economics**: The direct revenues and costs associated with a single unit of business activity (e.g., one service booking, one product sale).

**Valora**: A mobile wallet application FreClean recommends for sending, receiving, and holding USDm and CELO, including QR-code payments.

**White-label**: Manufacturing goods that a customer then sells under their own, different brand.
