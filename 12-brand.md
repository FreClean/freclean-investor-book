# Part XII: Brand

## Overview

FreClean's brand is, by a clear margin, the most mature and professionally developed part of the ecosystem examined in this book: a genuine asset, not a claim requiring heavy qualification.

## Brand Identity

The official FreClean logo is a circular badge combining a stylized house/roof silhouette, water droplets and sparkle elements, and a blue-to-green gradient ring, with the wordmark "Fre Clean" in a matching gradient. **(Confirmed**: the logo is used consistently across every FreClean repository and asset reviewed for this book; see [`../../assets/brand/`](../../assets/brand/) for a copy.)

## Brand Name and Story

The name **FreClean** combines "Fre" (from "Freda") and "Clean" (from the Haitian Creole "Pwòp," meaning clean at every level: conscious and economic). **(Confirmed**: provided directly by FreClean.) This etymology is a distinctive, ownable piece of brand storytelling rooted in FreClean's specific Haitian identity, not a generic global cleaning-brand name.

## Brand Promise

Expressed through FreClean's three-line tagline system: "Professional Cleaning Solutions • Premium Cleaning Products • Sustainable Innovation"; "Building Cleaner Spaces • Empowering Entrepreneurs • Creating Better Communities"; and "Clean Today. Sustain Tomorrow." **(Confirmed,** used consistently across FreClean's materials.)

## Positioning

FreClean positions itself explicitly around Haitian and Caribbean identity, sustainability, and entrepreneurship: not as a generic, placeless cleaning brand competing purely on price or convenience. This is a coherent, differentiated positioning relative to the competitor categories described in Part IX.

## Visual Identity

A derived color palette (built by direct pixel-sampling of the official logo, since no official color specification exists) centers on a blue (~#0A6ECA) to green (~#21975D–#4FA022) gradient. **(Not Yet Verified** as an official design specification; see the `freclean-brand` repository for full detail and methodology; this is a faithful reading of the actual logo, not an invented palette.) No official typeface, logo vector source files, or full brand guideline document exist yet beyond what the `freclean-brand` repository establishes as a first draft.

## Customer Perception

**(Not Publicly Disclosed / TBD.)** No customer survey, brand-awareness study, or sentiment data has been published. This book does not include invented testimonials or satisfaction claims.

## Brand Expansion Strategy

FreClean's brand is built with Caribbean and international expansion in mind (Part XIII): the tagline system and visual identity use plain international English and a globally legible visual style, rather than region-specific slang or imagery that would need to be redesigned for each new market. **(Assessment based on direct review of brand materials, not a FreClean-published strategy statement.)**
