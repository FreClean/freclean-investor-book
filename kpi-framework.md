# KPI Framework

Recommended KPIs for FreClean to begin tracking and reporting, per [`../book/chapters/19-investment-thesis.md`](../book/chapters/19-investment-thesis.md). None of these are currently published; this table is the tracking framework, not a reported dataset.

| KPI | Definition | Why it matters | Currently available? |
|---|---|---|---|
| Bookings per period | Number of service bookings completed per week/month | Core operating volume signal | No |
| Repeat-booking rate | % of customers who book again within a defined window | Validates retention (Part V) | No |
| Product-to-service attach rate | % of service customers who also purchase a product | Validates the ecosystem loop (Part VII) | No |
| Gross margin by revenue line | Margin for services vs. products vs. manufacturing | Validates the business model (Part VI, Part XIV) | No |
| Cash runway | Months of operation fundable at current burn | Core financial health signal | No |
| Active service area coverage | Confirmed cities/regions served | Grounds market-sizing (Part VIII) in reality | No |
| Products at "Active" lifecycle stage | Count of the 18 products actually in market (Part III) | Tracks product-line execution | No, currently 0 confirmed |
| Confirmed distributor/franchise partners | Count of signed, active partners (Part XIII) | Tracks distribution execution | No, currently 0 confirmed |
| USDm/CELO transaction share | % of payment volume through crypto rails (Part XI) | Tracks real adoption of the stated Web3 differentiator | No |

**Recommendation:** an investor engaging further with FreClean should request this table populated with real data as a first step, rather than proceeding on narrative alone.
