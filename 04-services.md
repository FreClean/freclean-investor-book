## Distribution of Services vs. Products

Unlike the product line (Part III), where no item has confirmed active-sale status, FreClean's service list is described with enough specificity (23 named, differentiated service types across three clear customer tiers) that it reads as an operating service menu rather than a purely aspirational list: though this book cannot independently confirm current transaction volume, staffing, or service-area coverage behind that menu. **(Assumption,** based on the specificity of the published list, not direct confirmation.)

## Services as the Entry Point to the Ecosystem

Services are the most plausible near-term revenue driver and customer-acquisition channel for FreClean, because they require less capital-intensive infrastructure (manufacturing, packaging, inventory) than the product line to begin generating revenue. This has a direct implication for Part VII, *Products + Services Ecosystem*: a first-time customer is more likely to encounter FreClean through a booked service than through a retail product purchase, at least until product distribution matures.
