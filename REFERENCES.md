## Market size: household/consumer cleaning products

Independent market-research firms report meaningfully different figures for the global household cleaning products market, reflecting different methodologies, product-scope definitions, and forecast years. This book treats the figures as an **order-of-magnitude range**, not a single precise number, and cites the range rather than picking one figure to make an argument look more precise than the underlying data supports.

- Precedence Research: global household cleaning market valued at approximately USD 247.73 billion in 2025, projected to reach USD 384.31 billion by 2034 (5% CAGR). *Household Cleaning Products Market Size to Surpass USD 384.31 Bn by 2034*, precedenceresearch.com, updated November 2025.
- Fortune Business Insights: global household cleaning products market valued at USD 282.28 billion in 2025, projected to reach USD 413.57 billion by 2034 (4.40% CAGR). *Household Cleaning Products Market Size*, fortunebusinessinsights.com, 2026.
- Global Market Insights: household cleaning products market valued at USD 215 billion in 2025, projected to reach USD 390 billion by 2035 (6.2% CAGR); North America accounted for USD 55.0 billion (25.6% of global share) in 2025. *Household Cleaning Products Market Size, Forecasts Report 2026–2035*, gminsights.com, 2026.
- ResearchAndMarkets (Global Industry Analysts): global market estimated at USD 189.0 billion in 2025, projected to reach USD 275.2 billion by 2032 (5.5% CAGR). *Household Cleaning Products – Global Strategic Business Report*, researchandmarkets.com, 2025.
- Coherent Market Insights, via GlobeNewswire: global cleaning products market valued at USD 163.45 billion in 2025, projected to reach USD 254.54 billion by 2032 (6.53% CAGR); commercial end-users cited as the largest segment. *Cleaning Products Market Size to worth USD 254.54 Billion by 2032*, GlobeNewswire, June 11, 2025.
- ResearchAndMarkets, via Yahoo Finance/GlobeNewswire: a narrower market definition placed the figure at USD 41.66 billion in 2025, projected to reach USD 61.50 billion by 2033 (4.99% CAGR). *Household Cleaners Market Competitive Landscape Report 2025*, GlobeNewswire, February 24, 2026.

**How this book uses these figures:** see [`../book/chapters/08-market.md`](../book/chapters/08-market.md) for the TAM/SAM/SOM framework built on this range. No single figure from the list above is presented as more authoritative than the others; the range itself (roughly USD 40 billion to USD 400+ billion depending on scope and year) is the honest takeaway.

## Haiti: macroeconomic and demographic data

- World Bank Group: Haiti's real GDP fell 2.7% in 2025, the seventh consecutive year of contraction; inflation averaged 28.3% in 2025; an estimated 49.0% of Haitians lived on less than US$3.00/day (2021 PPP) in 2025. *Haiti Overview*, worldbank.org, updated June 2026.
- World Bank Open Data: Haiti's total population was estimated at 11,906,095 in 2025, with population growth of 1.1% annually. *Haiti | Data*, data.worldbank.org.
- Trading Economics: Haiti's population was estimated at 11.90 million (December 2024 reading) with a 2025 projection of approximately 12.08 million. *Haiti Population*, tradingeconomics.com.
- Wikipedia (Economy of Haiti, citing IMF and national statistics): Haiti's nominal GDP was approximately USD 33.55 billion in 2025 (USD 38.2 billion at PPP); GDP per capita approximately USD 2,670 nominal (USD 3,040 PPP) in 2025; unemployment approximately 13.2%. *Economy of Haiti*, en.wikipedia.org, accessed 2026. (Note: other sources, including Macrotrends, cite a lower nominal GDP figure of approximately USD 24.26 billion for 2024: the range itself reflects differing data-collection methodologies for Haiti's economy, which the World Bank notes is subject to a large informal sector.)

## Haiti: diaspora remittances

- Haitian Institute of Statistics and Information (IHSI), via The Haitian Times: remittances to Haiti totaled USD 3.8 billion in 2023, roughly 20% of Haiti's GDP and the country's primary source of foreign currency. *Experts urge shift of Haitian Diaspora remittances toward investment*, haitiantimes.com, April 2024.
- Trading Economics (citing central bank data): remittances to Haiti rose to USD 4,111 million in 2024, up from USD 3,753.25 million in 2023. *Haiti Remittances*, tradingeconomics.com.

**How this book uses these figures:** diaspora remittance volume is cited in [`../book/chapters/08-market.md`](../book/chapters/08-market.md) and [`../book/chapters/13-distribution-and-expansion.md`](../book/chapters/13-distribution-and-expansion.md) as context for why a digital-payment-friendly, diaspora-aware business model may be relevant to FreClean's market: not as a claim that FreClean currently captures any share of this flow, which is unconfirmed.

All market-research figures above were current as of their respective publication dates (2025–2026) and are, by nature of the industry, subject to revision.
