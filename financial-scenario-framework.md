## Illustrative Scenario Comparison

| Scenario | Assumption | Directional implication |
|---|---|---|
| Conservative | Services-only revenue for 12+ months | Lower capital need; slower validation of the products ecosystem loop |
| Base | Services scale first; limited-SKU product launch within Year 1 | Matches FreClean's stated Phase 1 roadmap |
| Accelerated | Rapid franchise/wholesale signing alongside scaling | Higher capital need and execution risk |

**How to use this table:** replace every illustrative value with a real FreClean figure as it becomes available. The formulas (gross margin = price − direct cost; LTV = bookings × margin per booking) remain valid regardless of the inputs.
